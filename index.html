What every term in the control tower means

This glossary makes the prototype self-contained for interview reviewers. It defines the operating language behind the dashboard, scoring model, stages, rules, and investor narrative.

Top metrics

KPI definitions

Qualified pipeline
Modeled ACV from accounts above the priority threshold and not disqualified by hard-fit rules.
Partner share
The percentage of synthetic pipeline sourced through Google Cloud or channel partners.
Pilot to prod
The share of active, non-discovery accounts that are production candidates or commit-ready.
Average days to production
Modeled time-to-production across qualified accounts, used as a deployment-velocity signal.
Forecast cover
Weighted forecast divided by a synthetic quarterly target, used as a coverage proxy.
Escalations
Accounts with hard blockers or rule-triggered leadership flags.

Scoring model

Score definitions

Fit score
How well the opportunity matches AUI's likely wedge: policy-critical, auditable, action-heavy workflows with integration depth.
Readiness score
How credible the path to production is based on sponsor strength, security progress, integration readiness, success criteria, and resourcing.
Strategic value
Modeled importance beyond near-term ACV, including vertical proof, expansion potential, and partner leverage.
Blocker penalty
A deduction for unresolved risks such as security review, missing owner, weak measurement, product gap, or sponsor gap.
Priority score
The final rank: 50% fit, 35% readiness, 15% strategic value, minus blocker penalties.

Forecasting

Forecast terms

Commit
Revenue that clears strict readiness gates: sponsor, security, integration, stage, and success-criteria rules.
Best case
Strong opportunities that are plausible but still need one or more readiness signals before commit.
Pipeline
Active opportunities that remain valid but are not yet forecastable with high confidence.
At risk
Accounts with hard blockers, weak fit, missing ownership, low readiness, or poor measurement paths.
Weighted forecast
Estimated ACV multiplied by scenario weights for commit, best case, pipeline, and at-risk categories.

Production path

Stage definitions

Discovery
Business pain, owner, and fit are still being confirmed.
Pilot scoped
The pilot has a defined use case, but success criteria or implementation scope may still need tightening.
Security review
The account is blocked or gated by security, compliance, procurement, or risk approval.
Integration build
The technical path is active and depends on systems access, APIs, implementation ownership, and test data.
Production candidate
The account is close to launch and mainly needs final go-live alignment and support planning.
At risk
The account needs intervention or deprioritization before it consumes more GTM capacity.

Rules

Business-rule meanings

Commit requires success criteria
An opportunity cannot become commit without measurable production outcomes.
Boost regulated workflows
Accounts with policy-heavy, auditable, or regulated workflows get extra fit and strategic weight.
Escalate stale security review
Security review older than 14 days creates a leadership-visible blocker.
Down-rank weak-fit use cases
Low-stakes, open-ended, or low-measurement opportunities lose priority.
Cap readiness without owner
Production readiness cannot be trusted without a named integration owner.

Account fields

Data field definitions

Estimated ACV
Synthetic annual contract value for the modeled opportunity.
Policy criticality
How important rules, compliance, approvals, or governed decisions are to the workflow.
Process actionability
How much the agent must do, route, update, resolve, or trigger rather than just answer.
Auditability need
How important traceability and explainable decision records are for the buyer.
Integration readiness
How ready the customer and AUI would be to connect the systems required for production.
Sponsor strength
How clearly an executive or business owner can fund, unblock, and champion the deployment.

Narrative

Interview language

Traction
Evidence of demand, pipeline, expansion opportunity, or partner leverage.
Confidence
Evidence that pipeline can become production revenue: stage discipline, readiness, and forecast quality.
Leadership ask
The decision or unblock needed from executives this week.
Investor snapshot
A concise summary that tells investors what is growing, what is believable, and what risks are being managed.
